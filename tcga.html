TCGA reference

The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) is a foundational pan-cancer resource. It holds tumour and normal tissue from thousands of patients across many cancer types, with slide images and molecular measurements released through the NCI Genomic Data Commons. This page shows which TCGA patients have which measurements, which assays and patients we excluded and why, and how the analysis cohorts were defined.

General availability and the selected analysis panel

We report TCGA in two ways. Data availability is what each project provides after assay-quality exclusions: patients with RNA-seq, 450k methylation or both, and normal tissue with a slide image. The selected analysis panel is a selection for studies of tumour heterogeneity, which adds a patient treatment and diagnosis screen, histology-defined and pooled cohorts, and a minimum cohort size.

The panel's criteria serve one study design. Data outside it may suit other questions, including the patients withheld by the treatment screen and the 27k methylation array, which is not counted here. How the two views differ.

The size threshold

A cohort enters the panel only if it has enough patients for the planned RNA-seq and methylation analyses: at least 150 patients with a primary-tumour RNA-seq or 450k methylation profile, and at least 100 of them with both. Patients are counted once, after assay-quality exclusions and before the treatment and diagnosis screen.

  • It applies to the analysis cohort, not to the TCGA project. Colon and rectal adenocarcinoma form one colorectal cohort (CRC: 599 patients, 379 with both), and lower-grade glioma and glioblastoma one diffuse glioma cohort (GLIOMA: 756 and 565). TCGA-READ (156 and 94) and TCGA-GBM (242 and 51) would not pass on their own.
  • Where a project mixes distinct diseases, the cohort is the histology analysed. TCGA-ESCA contains oesophageal adenocarcinoma and squamous cell carcinoma; TCGA-CESC cervical squamous cell carcinoma and adenocarcinoma; TCGA-PAAD pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma and a few adenosquamous, colloid and other carcinomas. Their cohorts are oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC), cervical squamous cell carcinoma (CSCC) and pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC).
  • Two cohorts are explicit exceptions. ESCC has 90 patients, below both limits, and PDAC has 149, one patient below the total limit.
  • Sarcoma is not in the panel. TCGA-SARC passes as a project (225 patients, 223 with both), but it combines several distinct sarcoma types, and each is below the threshold: the largest, soft-tissue leiomyosarcoma, has 53 patients, and all leiomyosarcomas together 87.

Patient-level data

The TCGA patients view of the data explorer lists every patient in the panel projects: retained and excluded assays, the patient-screen decision and its reasons, histology and molecular annotations, and normal tissue. Any combination of these can be filtered and exported as CSV or JSON. Download links for the GDC files, PanCanAtlas matrices and quality annotations are on the sources page.

Tumour slide linkage and image QC have not yet been checked for the panel, and normal tissue comes from patients with cancer rather than healthy donors.
