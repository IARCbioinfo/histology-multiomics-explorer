Spatial studies

Published spatial omics studies show where molecular programmes and cell states sit within a tumour and how they relate to its morphology. They usually come from patients other than those in TCGA, so they add tissue context to the reference but no measurements for TCGA patients.

Verification and access

Matched data located
The original H&E image, measured expression and spatial coordinates were found for individual tissue sections, which are listed one by one in the data explorer. Image registration and tissue QC are still to be completed.
Verification pending
A catalogued study that may provide matched data but has an open check, such as original scans not located, an unconfirmed section relationship, an unresolved donor count or a pending histology review. Its eligible patients are not established.
Access-dependent
Data that are controlled, available on request, not yet released or without an established release route. Listing them does not mean that access has been requested or granted.

Each resource's details record how H&E and measurements are linked (same section, released together, serial section or unverified), the assay type, the tissue it comes from (primary, metastatic, adjacent-normal, normal or precursor) and treatment before sampling. Browse and export

The resource list in the data explorer shows every spatial study, with filters for assay type, H&E linkage, tissue, treatment and access. The spatial transcriptomics view lists every tissue section with located data, with its histology, tissue site, treatment before sampling and access, including a filter for untreated primary tumours. Both export the current selection as CSV or JSON.
