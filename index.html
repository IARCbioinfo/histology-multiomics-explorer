What we curated, and why

Building a research cohort requires more than finding available files. We review assay-quality annotations, treatment and diagnosis information, histological definitions and sample linkage. We document the resulting selections and uncertainties so that researchers can understand and adapt them.

Our current analysis panel was assembled for studies of tumour heterogeneity, including archetype analysis. Its selection criteria are explicit research choices; other questions may require different cohorts.

  • Molecular coverageRNA-seq and 450k methylation by patient, counted separately and together.
  • Assay and patient exclusionsQuality flags per aliquot, then treatment, diagnosis and specimen review per patient.
  • Histology decisions and poolingHistology-defined cohorts, pooled projects and sensitivity subsets.
  • Sample linkage and count unitsWhich images and measurements share a patient, sample or section, and what each count means.
